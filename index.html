Hoard

For the screenshots you took so you wouldn't have to remember

Find any screenshot by what's in it.

Hoard reads your screenshot pile with open AI models that run inside this browser tab. Search it by what you remember, let it gather the junk, and ask it questions. Your screenshots never leave this device.

The first run downloads about 250 MB of models, once. After that Hoard works with Wi‑Fi off.

  1. 1

    Search by memory

    "wifi password", "where did I park", "that sunset". Hoard reads every word on every screenshot and looks at the picture too.

  2. 2

    Sweep, don't sort

    Duplicates, dead one-time codes, expired boarding passes and accidental screenshots, gathered into one tidy pile. Nothing is ever deleted.

  3. 3

    Slow and steady

    Indexing runs quietly at sloth pace, picks up where it stopped, and catches new screenshots each time you come back.

Check for new screenshots? Your browser needs a click before Hoard can look in your folder again.
